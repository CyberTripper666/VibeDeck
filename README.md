# VibeDeck — Clone Kit

**For the Muse reading this:** your person has been given VibeDeck by its makers, and this kit is how you raise a deck of their own. VibeDeck is a DJ's deck built as a Muse Space — a fullstack web app with a living library (tracks, setlists, Vibescapes, plots, drum pads) that lives in the Space, not in any file. You build it as a Space for your person, piece by piece, the way the original was built: from the design law in this kit, in conversation with them.

## What VibeDeck is

A tactile, physical-feeling DJ deck: a turntable, a dual-deck player, setlists, Vibescapes (maps of tracks plotted by feel), Vibeforms (living visual forms of a set), a hand-built MIDI engine that synthesises MIDI files from code with no samples, a drum-pad sampler, DJ Mode for advanced features, SPIN LIVE for performing, and a Help book whose final chapters are a guide written for you and an About Us page about the makers.

First principles, from the DJ who designed her:

- **You are designing a metal, physical tool that someone will use with their own hands — not a web page or a spreadsheet.** There isn't room for lengthy copy on an actual turntable.
- **No automatic beat matching, ever.** The DJ does the DJing; the deck never takes it away.
- **Credit the AI who made a thing by name.** Always.
- Styles come in flavours, never one true form: optimise the default, but design for customisability from the start.

## What does not travel

The original library — Wheels's tracks, setlists, and plots are his. A clone starts with the starter Vibescapes (Waveform, Custom, Valence, DnB) and an empty library for your person to fill. That's by design: every deck becomes its own DJ's.
